# Anstoss terminology glossary (de ↔ en)

Source of truth for every locale. German is the fallback and the reference; English is the neutral international/UK reference for fr/it/pt/tr reviewers. Pick the listed term, use it everywhere, and do not mix synonyms across namespaces.

## Register and style
- German: always "du" (lowercase du/dein/dich), never "Sie". Short, warm, direct. German quotation marks „…“. Correct Umlauts and ß. Unmarked forms for people (Spieler, Trainer, Mitfahrer), no gender colons (":innen") and no "der/die"; use neutral phrasing where it reads naturally ("wer fährt", "Mitfahrende" only if it fits).
- English: British spelling (organise, colour, cancelled, favourite, licence as noun), sentence case for labels and buttons, "kick-off" (noun) / "kick off" (verb), "line-up".
- Buttons: a verb, max three words (DE "Speichern", "Termin anlegen"; EN "Save", "Add event").
- Errors: say what happened in plain words, then what to do next ("Das hat nicht geklappt. Versuch es gleich noch mal." / "That didn't work. Please try again.").
- Brand: the app is "Anstoss" (double s, never "Anstoß" as a name). "Anstoß" is the kick-off.
- Accepted anglicisms in German: Chat, Team, App, Link, Code, Admin, Check-in, Push (only as "Push-Benachrichtigung"). Avoid: Event, RSVP, Readiness, Briefing, Blocker, Nudge, Follow-up, No-Show, Staff, Update(s) (use Neuigkeiten/Änderungen), Dashboard (use Übersicht), Feed where a German word fits.
