Detailed worksheets already exist in `docs/launch/`. This page is the checklist that points to them.

| 8 | **Children's data lawful basis** | Global age gate (`AgeGateGuard` as `APP_GUARD`, rules in `apps/api/src/auth/age-gate.service.ts`) on every authenticated route. Routes with no club context need an approved consent somewhere. Only allow-listed routes (`@AllowWithoutConsent`: own profile, consent flows, account deletion, data export, onboarding, invite redemption, push token (un)register, session refresh) stay open to a blocked child. Pushes skip a child who fails the gate for that club. Guardians withdraw in the app (`app/child-consent.tsx`, `POST /consent/:id/withdraw`); withdrawal revokes the team access in the same transaction (under a per-child-per-team advisory lock) and detaches the child from future team activity (RSVPs and Kader nomination, carpool seats, lineups, open duties); the team's coaches and club admins are pushed, the guardian is always emailed. A club admin may withdraw only with a documented note; the guardian gets an email with that note and sees it under `/consent/mine`. Any guardian's new approval re-admits a child whose access was revoked by a withdrawal. A guardian email may not be the player's or a club staff member's. Deleting a guardian's account withdraws the consents they gave and closes their pending requests. Player loans of an under-16 need consent. | Guardian identity is only proven by email access. | 14 | **Data kept after consent withdrawal, and offboarding** | After a withdrawal the child keeps the account and the club membership; team access is `REVOKED`, future RSVPs, carpool seats, lineup spots and open duties are deleted, and the age gate blocks that team (and the club when no approved consent remains).
