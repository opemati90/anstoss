## 2. Definitions

We use the GDPR's terminology in this policy. In particular:
- **Personal data**: any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
- **Processing**: any operation performed on personal data (collection, recording, storage, retrieval, transmission, deletion).
- **Data subject**: the identified or identifiable natural person whose personal data is processed.

## 3. Data we collect

When you use Anstoss we process the following data:

### 3.1 Account and profile
- First and last name
- Date of birth (for age verification under GDPR Art. 8 — children under 16 require parental consent)
- Mobile phone number (for OTP login)
- Email address (optional)
- Profile picture (optional)
- Preferred app language

### 3.2 Club and team
- Club affiliation, role (Player, Coach, Parent, Club Admin)
- Team, position, jersey number
- Availability for training sessions and matches (RSVPs)

### 3.3 Communication
- The content of your chat messages in team, club, and direct chats
- Voice notes, image messages, reactions, shared content
- Detected language of your messages (for automatic translation into recipients' languages)
- Read receipts

### 3.4 Match operations
- Lineups, MOTM votes, injury reports (where you submit them)
- Linked club page on FUSSBALL.DE and imported match‑report data

### 3.5 Device and usage data
- Device model, OS version, app version
- Push tokens (Apple/Google) for delivery of push notifications
- IP address, request date and time
- Error and crash reports (anonymised via Sentry)

## 4. Legal bases for processing

- **Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR** (contract performance) — for everything required to deliver the app (account, team features, chat).
- **Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR** (legitimate interest) — for security logs, error diagnostics, anti‑abuse, product improvement.
- **Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR** (consent) — for push notifications, marketing emails, optional features such as public free‑agent visibility.
- **Art. 8 GDPR** (children under 16) — additional parental consent for accounts of users under 16.

## 6. Retention period

- **Account data**: until you delete your account.
- **Chat messages**: until deleted by you or a club admin.
- **Translation cache**: until the original message is deleted (cascaded).
- **Security/access logs**: 30 days.
- **Sentry error data**: 90 days.
- **Marketing consents and revocations**: up to 3 years after revocation.

We delete or anonymise personal data once the purpose of processing has lapsed and no statutory retention obligations apply.

## 7. International transfers

Where data is transferred to countries outside the EEA (e.g. to US sub‑processors), this is done on the basis of EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and supplementary safeguards (encryption, pseudonymisation).
