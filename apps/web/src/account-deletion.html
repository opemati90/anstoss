Account deletion / Konto löschen

Delete your Anstoss account

You can delete your account in the app or request deletion here. This page is the public deletion URL for App Store and Google Play review.

In the app

  • Open Anstoss and sign in.
  • Go to More → Data → Delete account.
  • Confirm the destructive action. Your session ends immediately.

Without app access

Email us from the email address attached to your Anstoss account. If you cannot access that mailbox, include enough information for us to verify ownership before deletion.

Email privacy@anstoss.io Privacy policy

What gets deleted

  • Account identity, profile fields, memberships, team access, push tokens, and sessions.
  • Personal chat, event, RSVP, lineup, and moderation references are deleted or anonymized.
  • Uploaded account-owned media is removed from storage where it is under our control.

What may be retained

  • Payment, invoice, tax, security, fraud-prevention, and legal records may be retained where required by law.
  • Aggregated, anonymized operational statistics may remain when they no longer identify you.

We process deletion requests within 30 days unless a longer legal retention duty applies.

Deutsch

Konto löschen

In der App: Mehr → Daten → Konto löschen. Ohne App-Zugriff sende eine E-Mail von der mit dem Konto verknüpften Adresse an privacy@anstoss.io.

Kontodaten, Profilfelder, Mitgliedschaften, Teamzugriffe, Push-Tokens und Sitzungen werden gelöscht oder anonymisiert. Zahlungs-, Steuer-, Sicherheits- und gesetzliche Nachweise können im erforderlichen Umfang aufbewahrt werden. Wir bearbeiten Anfragen innerhalb von 30 Tagen.
